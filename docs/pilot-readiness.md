# Municipal pilot readiness

This local prototype is suitable for a hackathon workflow demonstration, not public municipal operations.

## Select and prepare two or three real agenda items

Choose a land-use amendment requiring additional approval, a road redesign with documented consultation responses, and a budget/infrastructure decision with historical participation. For each, collect reports, agenda references, minutes, resolutions, conditions, amendments, notices, and authoritative geometry. An engagement landing page alone is insufficient.

Create a real project in Studio with the fictional checkbox cleared. Enter the actual sources and exact passages; add decisions, events, versions, themes, participation windows, and geography only where supported. No source should inherit a fictional date or extent. Check all source ownership/licensing and privacy obligations. Ask municipal staff to verify the procedural state and completeness.

## Required before external deployment

- Authentication, role-based authorization, independent reviewer identity and approval.
- Secure hosting, request limits, authenticated draft access, hardened headers and dependencies.
- Tamper-resistant audit history and historical publication retention.
- Backup/recovery exercises, migration procedures, document retention and change monitoring.
- Privacy review, data minimization, source attribution and accessibility audit.
- Authoritative geographic provenance and support for the geometry types needed by the chosen municipality.
- A verified method for participation-link freshness; a policy for expired or contradictory evidence.

## Evaluation plan

Have residents discover a project, distinguish its current stage, locate a condition, compare versions, inspect a passage, interpret geographic relevance, and identify a verified next step. Have staff create a case, correct a claim, inspect source text, verify dates, publish, and confirm the resident snapshot. Record errors, task completion, misunderstood language, reviewer agreement, and source-link correctness.

Current checks are automated software tests and development review only. No resident study, municipal legal verification, or formal WCAG 2.2 AA audit has been completed. Do not present fictional cases as validated real municipal examples.

## Future development priorities

First improve verified source collection and update procedures. Then add independent review roles, publication history, accessible document ingestion, official map datasets, and resident usability refinements. AI-assisted extraction may follow only with source-constrained outputs, explicit drafts, validation, and mandatory human approval. Predictive traffic, property, or environmental models are outside this prototype.
